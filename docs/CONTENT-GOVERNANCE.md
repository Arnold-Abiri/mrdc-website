# Content Governance Guide

Role/function-based (no named officers — assignments are a council task).

## Responsibilities
- **Authors** (department staff): prepare drafts with approved wording, real
  dates/references, complete metadata (category, year, department, alt text).
- **Verifiers** (department heads / delegated reviewers): check accuracy,
  approvals, privacy (no personal data in public fields), then mark
  Verified/Publishable.
- **Publishers** (authorized publishers): publish, unpublish expired items,
  archive superseded ones.
- **ICT**: owns the mechanics (accounts, scopes, translations tooling,
  reports) — not content approval.

## Cadence
- News/notices: review weekly; expired notices withdraw automatically but
  verify quarterly.
- Tenders/vacancies: close automatically by date; awards recorded promptly
  with cleared details only.
- Officials/wards/contacts: review on any personnel or boundary change.
- Projects: progress updated only with council-managed figures.
- Rates/financials: per approved publication cycle; replacements (not
  duplicates) with history preserved.
- Translations: English first; sn/nd added when approved; Translation status
  reviewed monthly.

## Rules
Expired, closed, superseded, or unapproved content must not present as
current. Demo/unverified material must never be published as production.
Document replacement preserves history — never delete-and-recreate to “fix”
a file. Audit records are never edited to hide mistakes.
