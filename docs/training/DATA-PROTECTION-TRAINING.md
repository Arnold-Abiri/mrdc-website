# Data Protection Training (operational responsibilities)

Aligned to Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act compliance requirement.
This is operational guidance, not legal advice — final wording and policy
approval sit with council/legal (`privacy-policy` page is a draft for review).

1. **Scope**: enquiries, complaints, investor enquiries, contact details,
   analytics aggregates, logs, audit events, uploaded documents.
2. **Necessity**: collect only what each form asks for; do not request or
   store extra personal data “just in case”.
3. **Non-publication**: citizen submissions are never public CMS content and
   never appear in search, sitemap, downloads, or analytics details.
4. **Separation**: never copy private submission content into public fields
   (pages, news, notices, documents, comments).
5. **Access control**: department scopes and role permissions restrict who
   sees what; internal notes stay staff-only; audit shows who accessed what.
6. **Analytics/logs**: aggregates only (no names/emails); raw analytics pruned
   by retention policy; audit logs follow separate governance retention and
   are never pruned to hide mistakes.
7. **Uploads**: redact personal data from published documents where required
   before publication.
8. **Disclosure**: suspected accidental disclosure → notify ICT immediately;
   record and contain per the incident procedure; do not attempt to delete
   audit traces.
